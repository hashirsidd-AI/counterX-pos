# counterX POS — User Manual (Daily Operations)

The left sidebar (dark blue) lists all screens. Click a name to open it. Your daily work happens mostly on **Dashboard**, **New Sale**, **Cash Register**, and **Shift Management**.

## 1. Log in

1. Open **counterX POS** from the Desktop or Start Menu.
2. Enter your **username** and **password**.
3. Click **Login**.

Different users see different screens. A cashier sees fewer options than an owner. This is controlled in **Employees & Permissions**.

## 2. Open a shift (start of day)

A shift must be open before you can make sales.

1. Open **Shift Management**.
2. Click **Open Shift**.
3. Enter the **opening cash** — count the notes and coins in the drawer and type the total.
4. Click **Open**.

The **Dashboard** will now show your open shift and today's figures.

## 3. Make a sale — the New Sale screen

1. Open **New Sale**.
2. Choose the order type: **Dine-In**, **Takeaway**, **Delivery**, or **Online**.
   - For Dine-In, enter the **table number** and **waiter name** if you use them.
3. Add products to the cart (right side of the screen):
   - **Type** in the search box — search works by product name, SKU, or barcode. Press **Enter** to add the first match.
   - Or click a **category** on the left, then click the product card.
   - A barcode scanner works like a fast keyboard: click the search box first, then scan.
4. Change a line with **+ Qty** / **− Qty**, **Edit Price** (if your role allows it), **Item Discount**, or **Remove**.
5. The cart shows **Subtotal**, **Discount**, **Tax**, and the **Total** automatically. Tax rates are set in **Business Settings**.

## 4. Hold and resume a sale

If a customer needs to wait:

1. Click **Hold**. The sale is parked safely.
2. To bring it back, click **Resume** and pick the held sale from the list.

Held sales are never lost, even if the app is closed.

## 5. Checkout (taking payment)

1. In **New Sale**, click **Checkout**.
2. Choose the payment method:
   - **Cash** — type the amount the customer gives in **Amount Tendered**. The screen shows the **change** to return.
   - **Card** — type the card amount.
   - **Bank Transfer** — type the transferred amount.
   - **Split** — combine methods (for example, part cash + part card).
3. Click **Complete** (the confirm button).

Each sale gets a unique **invoice number**. Double-clicking the button cannot create a duplicate sale — the app protects against this.

## 6. Receipts and kitchen tickets

- After checkout, click **Print Receipt** to print the customer receipt.
- Click **Print KOT** to print the **Kitchen Order Ticket** (shows table, waiter, and items for the kitchen).
- To reprint an old receipt, open **Orders & History**, find the sale, and click **Print Receipt**.

If no printer is connected, the app will save the receipt as a text file instead and tell you where it saved it.

## 7. Returns, refunds, and voids

These need a manager or owner — a cashier will see a "permission denied" message.

- **Refund** (customer returns an item): open the sale in **Orders & History** and click **Refund**. Enter the amount and reason. The refund is recorded and stock is returned.
- **Void** (cancel a completed sale by mistake): open the sale and click **Void**. The sale is marked voided — it is never deleted, so records stay honest.

## 8. Cash Register — cash in, cash out, expenses

1. Open **Cash Register**.
2. **Cash In** — money added to the drawer (for example, extra float from the owner).
3. **Cash Out** — money taken from the drawer (for example, paying a supplier in cash).
4. **Record Expense** — shop expenses (electricity, tea, repairs). Enter the category, amount, and a note.

Every cash movement is logged with your username and time.

## 9. Close the shift (end of day)

1. Open **Shift Management** and click **Close Shift**.
2. The screen shows the **Expected** cash — what the drawer *should* contain, calculated from your opening cash, cash sales, cash in/out, expenses, and refunds. Card payments never affect this number.
3. Count the physical cash in the drawer and type it into **Counted**.
4. The **Variance** shows shortage (negative) or overage (positive).
5. Click **Close Shift**, then **Print Report** for the closing slip.

Once closed, a shift's records are locked. Any later correction needs an owner and is logged.

## 10. Day Closing

Open **Day Closing** at the end of the business day to review the full day across all shifts: total sales, payment breakdown, and cash position. Print it for your records.

## 11. Reports & Analytics

Open **Reports & Analytics** to see:

- Daily and date-range sales
- Cashier performance
- Product-wise and category-wise sales
- Payment-method breakdown
- Discounts, returns, and tax summaries
- Stock value and low-stock items
- Estimated gross profit

Reports can be printed or exported. **Profit figures are estimates** — the app labels them as such because not every cost may be recorded.

## 12. Products, categories, and stock — the basics

- **Products & Menu** — add or edit products: name (English and Urdu), price, cost, barcode/SKU, category. Changes apply immediately.
- **Categories & Modifiers** — organize the menu; add sizes and add-ons (for example, "Large", "Extra Cheese").
- **Inventory & Stock** — see current stock, adjust quantities, and view the movement history of every item.
- **Purchases** — record stock received from a **Supplier**.
- Low-stock items appear on the **Dashboard** so you can reorder in time.

## 13. Customers and suppliers

- **Customers** — save regular customers' names and phone numbers to attach them to sales.
- **Suppliers** — save supplier details used in **Purchases**.

## Quick daily routine

1. Log in → 2. **Open Shift** (count opening cash) → 3. Sell on **New Sale** →
4. Record expenses in **Cash Register** → 5. **Close Shift** (count cash, print report) → 6. Review **Day Closing**.
