## How licensing works (plain words)

counterX POS is licensed **per shop computer**. One license key covers one computer.

The license is checked **offline, on your own computer**. The app never needs the internet to verify your license, and it never sends your sales data anywhere. Billing works with or without a license server, a connection, or an account.

## Activating your license

1. Open **License & About** from the sidebar.
2. Type your license key into the box (keys look like `CX1-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX`).
3. Click **Activate**.
4. The **License Status** should now show **Active**.

Keep your license key written down somewhere safe — for example, taped inside the USB stick case.
