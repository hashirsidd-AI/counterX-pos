# counterX POS v1.1.0 — Release Notes

**For shop owners, restaurant owners, and cashiers.**

## What's new

**Easier product entry.** Adding a product now takes seconds: name,
category, price, stock — done. Extra options (SKU, Urdu name, tax,
description) are tucked under "Advanced Options". You can even create a
new category right from the product form.

**Sample fast-food menu included.** On first setup you can load a ready
Pakistani fast-food menu (burgers, pizza, roast, fries, cold drinks —
21 items with prices and stock). Everything is marked as sample data and
can be edited or removed with one click. Or start empty — your choice.

**Better billing screen.** Categories are now easy buttons across the
top, products show as neat cards, and out-of-stock items are marked.
Table/waiter/charges are tucked into an "Order details" section so the
till stays clean.

**Better dashboard.** See today's sales, orders, average bill, cash in
the shift, low-stock items, and held orders at a glance — plus a 7-day
sales chart and your latest transactions.

**Professional CounterX branding** on the login screen, sidebar, and
receipts ("Powered by CounterX").

## Safe upgrade

- Installs over v1.0.0 **without deleting** your products, stock,
  sales, users, or settings.
- Your database is backed up automatically before any upgrade step.
- Nothing about billing, taxes, discounts, shifts, day closing,
  printing, backups, or cloud sync has changed — everything works
  exactly as before.
