# counterX POS — Installation Guide

## What you need

1. A computer with **Windows 10 or Windows 11** (64-bit).
2. The counterX USB stick.
3. About 10 minutes.

No internet connection is needed. Not for installation, and not for daily use.

## Step 1 — Run the installer

1. Plug the USB stick into the computer.
2. Open the USB stick and double-click **counterX-POS-Setup-1.0.0.exe**.
3. If Windows shows a blue "Windows protected your PC" message, click **More info**, then click **Run anyway**. This is normal for new software.
4. Click **Next** through the installer screens.
5. Keep the tick mark on **"Create a desktop shortcut"**.
6. Click **Install**, then click **Finish**.

## Step 2 — Open the app

You can open counterX POS in two ways:

- Double-click the **counterX POS** icon on the Desktop, or
- Open the **Start Menu** and click **counterX POS**.

## Step 3 — First-run setup (only the first time)

The first time the app opens, a setup wizard will guide you:

1. **Create the owner account.** Choose a username and a strong password. The owner can do everything in the app, so keep this password safe and private.
2. **Enter your business details:** business name, address, city, and phone number. These appear on receipts.
3. Click **Finish**.

Your database is created automatically at this point. You do not need to install or configure any database yourself.

## Step 4 — Log in

Every time the app opens, you will see the login screen. Enter your username and password, then click **Login**.

## Where your data lives

All your sales, products, and settings are stored on this computer only, in:

```
%APPDATA%\counterX
```

(For example: `C:\Users\YourName\AppData\Roaming\counterX`)

- Do **not** delete or move files in this folder.
- Each computer keeps its own separate data. If you install on two computers, they do **not** share data in this version.
- See **BACKUP_RESTORE_GUIDE.md** to learn how to protect this data.

## Uninstalling

You can uninstall from Windows **Settings → Apps**. Uninstalling removes the program but **keeps your business data** in the folder above, so a re-install will find your data again.
